Hydrogen molecule energy (VQE)

How much energy does an H₂ molecule have when its two atoms sit a given distance apart? At each distance a variational quantum eigensolver (VQE) tunes one rotation angle on a two-qubit circuit until the measured energy stops falling. The lowest point of the curve is the bond length. Hartree–Fock, the classical starting guess, is drawn for comparison.

Minimal STO-3G basis, ideal noiseless simulation. The circuit can reach every state this basis allows for the electron pair, so VQE lands on the exact answer here. The interesting part is how far Hartree–Fock drifts as the bond stretches. On real hardware, noise would open a gap.
